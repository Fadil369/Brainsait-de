Executive Summary

Five strategic blueprints covering enterprise architecture, governance, compliance, agentic AI, and go-to-market. Each blueprint is tied to an article of Directive BSD-2026-07 and is executable immediately.

This is not theory. Every section has been validated against a live production stack.

Blueprint 1 — Enterprise Architecture

Directive reference: Article 2

The enterprise is a network of business units, not a hierarchy of departments. Six domains operate under one identity:

| Domain | Mandate |

|---|---|

| brainsait.de | Flagship, healthcare AI |

| brainsait.cloud | Cloud, APIs |

| brainsait.de | Europe |

| elfadil.com | Founder platform |

| vip.elfadil.com | Premium |

| momfood.de | Adjacent commerce |

Rule: No domain is an island. Every domain exposes identity, catalog, and APIs to the Enterprise Intelligence Layer.

Blueprint 2 — Governance

Directive reference: Article 0

Governance is a document, not a meeting. The directive:

  • Establishes authority and supersession rules.
  • Lists every business unit and agent family.
  • Carries enforcement teeth (mandatory fields, registration requirements, audit hooks).

BrainSAIT's BSD-2026-07 is 16 articles, ~12 KB Markdown, generates HTML+PDF+EPUB, registered under OID 1.3.6.1.4.1.61026.10.1. The directive governs; the auditor verifies.

Blueprint 3 — Compliance

Directive reference: Article 6

Compliance is non-negotiable. For Saudi healthcare:

  • NPHIES — every claim validated against the CHI conformance suite.
  • FHIR R4 — every clinical resource exported with Saudi extensions.
  • PDPL — patient data stored in-region; consent ledger anchored to enterprise identity.

Compliance is not a checkbox; it is an attribute of every product in the catalog. The compliance block on each product records which standards it satisfies.

Blueprint 4 — Agentic AI

Directive reference: Article 7

The enterprise is Agent-First. Where a workflow can be done by a governed AI agent, it must be.

Recognized agent families (current):

| Agent | Domain |

|---|---|

| MASTERLINC | Orchestration |

| ClinicalLinc | Clinical decisions |

| ClaimLinc | Revenue cycle |

| PolicyLinc | Compliance |

| RadioLinc | Imaging |

| TTLINC | Training |

Each agent registers under OID arc .8 (Master AI) or its domain arc. Each agent carries AI Metadata (model, version, validation set, override path).

Blueprint 5 — Go-to-Market

Directive reference: Articles 4, 8

Distribution is the marketplace. Four categories: Software, Digital Products, APIs, Services.

Each product carries:

  • bilingual name and description (AR + EN)
  • SAR + USD pricing
  • schema.org structured data with OID brand identifier
  • marketplace listing URL
  • cross-references to the registry node

The marketplace is the catalog is the registry is the directive. They are one system.

How to Use These Blueprints

  • Pick the blueprint that maps to your immediate work.
  • Read the directive article it references (BSD-2026-07).
  • Apply the executable artifact — the script, the schema, the template.
  • Verify with the auditor — scripts/audit.py exits non-zero on deviation.
  • Register the outcome — every artifact gets an OID node and a changelog entry.

Why This Works

Because it is the same playbook used to ship the actual BrainSAIT ecosystem. Every blueprint has a working artifact behind it. Every directive article has a passing audit check. Every marketplace product has a registered OID.

The blueprints are not aspirational. They are operational.
