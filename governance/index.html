0Authority, Scope & Supersession

0.1 Issued under the authority of the Founder & CEO, this directive is the single source of truth for the strategic direction of the BrainSAIT ecosystem.

0.2 It binds all personnel, contractors, partners, AI agents, autonomous systems, and software services operating under Enterprise OID 1.3.6.1.4.1.61026.

0.3 Where any prior planning note, roadmap, or informal agreement conflicts with this directive, this directive prevails.

0.4 AI agents shall treat this document as governing context; contradicting outputs are non-compliant by definition.

0.5 Amendments require a new versioned directive issued by the Founder & CEO.

1Vision

BrainSAIT is no longer a standalone healthcare software company. It is an integrated, AI-native enterprise network — interconnected platforms, marketplaces, digital products, infrastructure, education, healthcare, logistics, publishing, and autonomous agents operating as one intelligent system.

The network shares nine substrates: Identity · Knowledge · Products · APIs · AI Agents · Enterprise Registry (129 registered nodes, 99 relationships as of 20 July 2026) · Marketplace · Authentication · Automation. Every unit builds on them, not around them.

3Enterprise Identity & Registry

The enterprise hierarchy expands under OID 1.3.6.1.4.1.61026. The Enterprise Registry is the authoritative record of organizations, products, APIs, AI agents, standards, clinical modules, digital assets, publications, courses, and certifications.

An asset that is not registered does not officially exist. Registration precedes launch. The baseline of record is the Intelligence Graph generated 20 July 2026.

4Intelligence Marketplace

The Marketplace is the primary distribution platform; unlisted direct distribution is deprecated. Categories: Software (SaaS, AI Assistants, LINC Agents, Healthcare Platforms, Enterprise Portals) · Digital Products (Books, Mini Books, PDF Collections, Courses, Templates, Prompt Packs, Clinical Documents) · APIs (Healthcare, Authentication, AI, NPHIES, Enterprise) · Services (Consulting, AI Transformation, Medical Coding, Training, Enterprise Deployment).

5Knowledge Products

All strategic documents shall be converted into premium digital products — Strategic AI Blueprints, Saudi Healthcare Transformation, Solo AI Business Blueprint, API Revision Notebook, Digital Organization Handbook, DOM Notes, Medical Coding Resources, Prompt Libraries, Research Reports, White Papers.

Format mandate: PDF · Markdown · EPUB · HTML · Interactive Version · Marketplace Listing. Missing formats = not shipped.

6Healthcare Ecosystem

Healthcare remains the clinical core: HNH, Basma, Clinical AI, FHIR, HL7, NPHIES, Revenue Cycle, Saudi SBS, Clinical Documentation, Medical Coding, Healthcare Analytics. NPHIES/FHIR conformance and PDPL-aligned data handling are non-negotiable.

8BrainSAIT Academy

The official education platform. Curriculum: Healthcare AI, Medical Coding, Programming, Python, Swift, Cloud, Automation, Enterprise Architecture, AI Development. Every course ships in Arabic and English.

9Publishing

A strategic business unit, not a side activity: Republic of Cells, Saudi Novel Initiative, Medical Books, AI Books, Technical Documentation, Storytelling, Interactive Learning — offered in multiple digital formats per Article 5.

10MOM Ecosystem

MOM Food expands beyond catering: Community Marketplace, Talent Platform, Recipes, Home Chefs, Events, Food Logistics, Delivery, Commerce — adopting the shared substrates of Article 1.

11Infrastructure

Standard stack: Cloudflare, Docker, Kubernetes-ready architecture, AI Gateways, MCP, Enterprise APIs, Authentication, Workers, Pages, Object Storage, Enterprise Monitoring. Deviations require documented approval.

12Product Standardization

Every product carries: Product ID · Enterprise OID · Documentation · Version · Changelog · Marketplace Listing · API Reference (if applicable) · Licensing · AI Metadata. Missing any element = pre-release by definition.

13Automation

Manual workflows transition progressively to autonomous execution. Priorities: content creation, customer onboarding, clinical workflows, product publishing, documentation, internal operations, marketplace management — always under human executive oversight.

14Documentation Policy

Every project maintains: Executive Summary, Technical Docs, API Docs, Deployment Guide, Architecture Diagram, User Guide, Admin Guide, AI Agent Specification, Release Notes. Documentation is a release gate.

15Immediate Priorities

  • 1. Registry consolidation — against the 20 July 2026 Intelligence Graph baseline
  • 2. OID hierarchy completion — all active units, products, agents
  • 3. Marketplace expansion — digital products, software, services
  • 4. Domain unification — six core domains, one identity & navigation experience
  • 5. Knowledge productization — strategic documents as premium offerings
  • 6. Healthcare & agents — healthcare AI platforms, LINC agents, enterprise automation
