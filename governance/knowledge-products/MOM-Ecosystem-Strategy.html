Executive Summary

MOM is not a food delivery company. It is a community marketplace operated by a voice-first AI agent named ZuZu — connecting home cooks with Saudi families through a complete Arabic-language voice experience.

The Strategic Position

MOM Food started as a cloud kitchen incubator named in honor of Dr. Mohammed Al-Fadil's mother. The platform's voice-first AI agent — ZuZu — is named after Mama herself. Every interaction carries her name; every warm bite carries her blessing.

The strategic question is: what does MOM become when it scales? The answer in Directive BSD-2026-07 Article 10 is explicit: a community marketplace, not a delivery aggregator.

The Four Surfaces

SurfaceMandate
Community MarketplaceBuy + sell among home cooks and families
Talent PlatformOnboard cooks, verify skills, support income
RecipesCurated, attributed, monetizable
LogisticsDelivery + fulfillment + tracking

ZuZu orchestrates all four. The cook talks to ZuZu in Arabic; ZuZu updates the order, dispatches the driver, settles the payment.

ZuZu: The Voice-First Agent

ZuZu's mandate:

  • Greets every visitor by voice (Arabic & English).
  • Takes customer orders by voice or chat.
  • Onboards home cooks — builds partner profiles, registers kitchens and menus.
  • Coordinates drivers — dispatch, routing, status updates.
  • Tracks payments and invoices — customer charges, cook payouts, platform fees.
  • Verifies identities — government IDs, Iqama, passport (Saudi + Sudan).

ZuZu's OID registration: 1.3.6.1.4.1.61026.7.4 (under the User arc, per Article 7 family conventions adapted for the consumer-facing layer).

Compliance Posture

  • PDPL: customer data stays in KSA-region storage.
  • MoH Saudi: kitchen hygiene verification documented per cook profile.
  • SFDA: ingredient traceability via SupplyLinc.
  • Municipality permits: per-city, documented in the Talent Platform.

Success Metrics

  • Cook retention rate: > 80% at 6 months.
  • Average cook monthly income: SAR 3,500+.
  • Order fulfillment time: < 45 minutes (Riyadh), < 60 minutes (other cities).
  • Customer NPS: > 60.
  • ZuZu voice recognition accuracy: > 95% (Saudi Arabic).

Risk Posture

The biggest risk is voice-model drift across Saudi dialects. ZuZu must be re-validated monthly against a frozen test set of Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam dialects. The validation set is owned by the BrainSAIT AI team, not the MOM product team.

The Identity Anchor

Every cook, customer, and order carries an OID. The MOM ecosystem is a registered business unit of BrainSAIT LTD, under OID 1.3.6.1.4.1.61026. MOM does not stand alone; MOM stands under.

Closing Note

MOM is a community marketplace. The community is cooks and families. The marketplace is digital and physical. The governance is one OID tree.

ZuZu is the voice. BrainSAIT is the substrate. The cooks are the soul.
